Q1 Tower

Standing 322.5 metres tall on the Gold Coast, the Q1 Tower is a iconic, 78-storey residential skyscraper inspired by the 2000 Sydney Olympic torch and features the famous SkyPoint observation deck.

Melbourne Cricket Ground(MCG)

The Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG) is Australia’s largest sports stadium, boasting a capacity of over 100,000 and serving as the iconic home to the AFL Grand Final and the Boxing Day Test.
